A LITTLE ABOUT US

Marketing should feel like you.

My interest in filming, producing and editing began while studying Creative Media. That creative foundation grew into hands-on experience as a Marketing Assistant, working with brands with different products, audiences and needs.

It taught me something important: a trend isn't a strategy, and what works for one brand won't automatically work for another.

Pop Reel Studio was created to help small businesses, brands and individuals feel more confident online — especially when video, posting publicly or staying consistent feels daunting.

First, we learn your brand. Then we create with purpose.
